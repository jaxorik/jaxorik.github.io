Common Government Contracting Challenges

Procurement Complexity

Black Box AI Risks

"We can't deploy AI systems we can't explain or audit for mission-critical government operations."

Every AI system we build is transparent, auditable, and explainable. We do not make black box AI. Our development process aligns with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework. Read our primer on explainable AI →

Vendor Reliability

"We need contractors who understand government timelines, budgets, and won't disappear mid-project."

We're veterans with deep roots in government contracting. We're not a venture-backed shop chasing the next pivot. We bid on projects we'd be proud to deliver, and we deliver on time.

Technical Integration

"New AI systems need to integrate with our existing government IT infrastructure and security protocols."

We deliver models on a fresh machine pre-configured to your environment, unless a different delivery method is established in advance.

Classified Data Concerns

"We need AI tools but our data is classified and we don't want vendors in our facility."

We ship you an unclassified, ready-to-run model on a configured laptop. Your team loads your data on your systems. The model never sees classified information — the classification boundary stays where it belongs.

Transparent AI for Government Applications

Government agencies can't afford to deploy AI systems they can't explain, audit, or trust. Black box algorithms create security risks, compliance nightmares, and operational failures.

We build AI systems designed for government accountability. Every decision is auditable, every method is documented, and every result is reproducible.
