Common Emergency Response Challenges

"We need to know where to send resources, but our current damage assessment takes weeks, and we need answers today."

Faster Damage Assessment

Damage assessment from satellite and drone imagery is one of the problems I'm working toward: faster reports, even with partial, corrupted, or cloud-obscured imagery. This work is in development.

"Our cloud-based tools don't work when cell towers are damaged and the internet is unreliable."

Offline-Ready Tools

The models are designed to run completely offline on laptops and field devices. No internet, no cloud, no subscriptions. Field conditions often mean no connection, so I'm building for that from day one.

"We're making resource deployment decisions based on incomplete information and best guesses."

Built for Incomplete Data

Standard models require clean data, which means they'd be trained to ignore exactly the conditions a crisis creates. My models are designed to handle missing, noisy, and corrupted inputs without generating false confidence.

"Our emergency coordinators need tools that work immediately without extensive technical training."

Designed for the Field

The goal is tools built for emergency managers, not computer scientists, delivered pre-configured and ready to accept your data.
